[![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

## FAQ

### What are AI search visibility tools?

AI search visibility tools track how often and how accurately your brand appears in responses generated by AI-powered answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews. Unlike traditional SEO tools that measure keyword rankings on a results page, AI visibility tools measure whether you appear *inside the AI-generated answer itself* - including which prompts surface you, how competitors appear alongside you, which pages earn your citations, and whether the sentiment is accurate.

### What is the difference between GEO and AEO?

**Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** is the practice of optimizing content so that generative AI engines (like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini) are more likely to cite and recommend your brand in their answers. **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** is the broader discipline of optimizing for any AI-powered answer engine, including Google AI Overviews, voice search, and featured snippets. In practice, the terms are often used interchangeably - both refer to making your content more visible and citable inside AI-generated responses rather than traditional search result pages.

### Which AI engines should I track?

At minimum: **ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews** - these three drive the most AI-influenced buying decisions today. Add **Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Mode** if your buyers use them. Research shows that 91% of cited URLs appear in just one LLM, so strong performance on ChatGPT tells you little about your Perplexity or Gemini visibility - multi-engine tracking is essential.

### What metrics do AI search visibility tools measure?

The core metrics across most platforms:
- **Brand mention rate** - how often your brand appears across a tracked set of prompts
- **Share of voice** - your mentions as a percentage of all brand mentions in your category
- **Citation rate** - how often your domain is cited as a source in AI answers
- **Sentiment** - whether AI describes your brand positively, neutrally, or negatively
- **Position / prominence** - whether you appear first, in a list, or as a footnote
- **Visibility by engine** - your mention rate broken down per AI platform (ChatGPT vs. Perplexity vs. Gemini, etc.)
- **Prompt coverage** - which buyer-intent prompts surface your brand vs. competitors

### How is AI search visibility different from traditional SEO rankings?

Traditional SEO tracks your position in a ranked list of blue links. AI visibility tracks whether you're mentioned inside a conversational answer - there is no "position 1" in the traditional sense. Key differences:
- AI responses are probabilistic and vary significantly run-to-run (SparkToro research found AI platforms produce identical brand recommendations less than 1 in 100 times)
- The key metric is **visibility percentage** (how often you appear across a prompt sample), not a rank position
- AI engines draw from citations and training data, not just keyword relevance
- Content that earns AI citations is often structured differently - factual, authoritative, directly answering questions

### Are there free AI search visibility tools?

Yes. Free options include:
- **AI Monitor** - fully open-source
- **AutoGEO** - open-source research framework (GitHub)
- **Trakkr.ai** - free beta with daily LLM tracking
- **Gumshoe AI** - free tier (3 full report runs)
- **AthenaHQ** - free visibility report
- **Quno.ai** - free tier with basic visibility scorecard

Most paid platforms also offer free trials: Frase (7 days), Nightwatch (14 days), Otterly.AI (7 days), Semrush (14 days).

### How do I improve my brand's visibility in AI answers?

The factors most consistently associated with higher AI citation rates:
1. **Earn citations on high-authority third-party sites** - AI engines heavily weight sources they trust (major publications, Wikipedia, industry sites)
2. **Structure content to directly answer questions** - FAQs, clear headings, factual statements AI can extract
3. **Maintain consistent brand information** - Name, description, and positioning should be consistent across your site, press releases, and third-party mentions
4. **Optimize for the prompts your buyers actually use** - Use a tool like Profound, Nightwatch, or Frase to discover which prompts surface your category, then create content that answers them
5. **Ensure AI crawlers can access your site** - Check your robots.txt and server logs for GPTBot, Anthropic-ai, PerplexityBot, and Google-Extended
6. **Build a content moat in your niche** - AI engines favor brands with depth on a topic, not breadth across many

### What should I look for when choosing an AI search visibility tool?

- **Engine coverage** - does it track the engines your buyers use?
- **Prompt scale** - can it handle thousands of prompts, or just dozens?
- **Sentiment + context** - does it show *how* AI talks about you, not just *if*?
- **Citation source attribution** - which pages earn your citations?
- **Actionability** - does it tell you what to do next, or just show a dashboard?
- **Monitor vs. fix** - some tools only alert you; others (like Frase, AIclicks) connect monitoring to a content creation workflow
- **Agency support** - if you manage multiple brands, look for multi-client workspaces, pitch environments, and white-label options

### What is share of voice in AI search?

AI share of voice measures what percentage of AI-generated responses in your category mention your brand, relative to all brand mentions. If AI mentions your brand in 30 out of 100 relevant prompts, and mentions competitors in the remaining 70, your AI share of voice is 30%. Platforms like Profound, Otterly.AI, Semrush AI Toolkit, and Nightwatch all track this metric and let you benchmark it against specific competitors over time.
